Every vessel of this eye, as the slit-lamp camera sees it: each absorbs light by its calibre, haematocrit and depth over the scleral texture, and its red cells move at their simulated speed, pulsing with the heart. All levels of detail are one solved network; zooming in reveals the finer vessels as a finer camera would. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom toward the cursor, right-drag to pan.
